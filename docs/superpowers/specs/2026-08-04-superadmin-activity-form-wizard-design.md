# Superadmin Activity Form Wizard

Create and Edit Activity use a responsive four-step presentation flow: Identity, Structure and locations, Links, and Professionals and review. This delivery emits structured drafts through asynchronous callbacks and does not persist or authorize data.

Draft save requires name, institution, and at least one unit; completion also requires at least one class. Internal locations are optional session drafts owned by a selected unit. Professional assignments belong to activity plus class; Happens, Now, Moments, and Chat start enabled, while effective authorization remains subject to institutional hierarchy. Editing keeps institution fixed and preserves legacy `Fixa` governance as read-only.

Supabase, migrations, schema, repository writes, effective permissions, and changes to Groups or Units are out of scope.
